Market dynamics continue to evolve rapidly.    Regulatory  > Opana ER removal represents the first time the FDA has requested the removal of a product from the market for    illicit use and potentially sets a precedent for further action  > Continued focus on abuse deterrent formulations by the FDA  Payors  > Limited to no distinction between branded opioids and generics  > Reduced prescription duration  > Movement towards non-opioids as first-line treatment  Government / Policy  > Government scrutiny continues to put downward pressure on TRx trends  > DOJ focused on opioids and potential actions against entire value chain (manufacturers, distributors and    prescribers)  > Actions such as California’s Drug Pricing Transparency Bill may dampen pharma’s ability to use price as a lever  Competitors  > Ability to actively promote branded opioid products has been reduced thereby limiting the market opportunity  > Companies are electing to refrain from launching opioid-related products (only 6 of 9 approved ADF-opioid    products have been launched)  > While we do expect additional branded entrants, the return profile has changed such that most companies    investing in branded opioid development are unlikely to generate long-term returns  > Product development hindered by higher barriers to entry
