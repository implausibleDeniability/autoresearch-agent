Project Memphis Situation Overview
Memphis has proposed a JV to commercialize a portfolio of assets for the Hospital channel
Purdue would lead all commercialization and R&D efforts, initiating with 50 FTE's and building to 150 FTE's in 3 years (by 2017)

Pain products:
Ionsys (US approved): transdermal mu-opioid agonist
ABP-700 (NDA in 2017): injectable Gamma-aminobutyric acid (GABA) A modulator
Anti-infectives:
Orbactiv (US launch Oct 2014): First and only single-dose treatment for ABSSSI
Carbavance: (Launch in 2017): IV fixed-dose combination antibiotic
Minocin IV (Launched 2009, reformulation scheduled to launch July 2015): approved for treatment of infections caused by Acinetobacter spp.

Current SOC is either IV PCA of morphine or fentanyl and has several drawbacks:
 ~50,000 patients  per year are harmed due to PCA errors (79% attributed to device errors)
