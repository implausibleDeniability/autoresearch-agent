ADF Strategy Context
Purdue is the market leader in opioids and has heavily invested in developing market leading abuse deterrence technology and capabilities for their products (OxyContin, HYD)

While the current environment does not support premium pricing for abuse deterrent technology, regulatory scrutiny on opioids and opioid abuse has increased
The launch of Zohydro has fueled additional public interest in abuse of opioids

An ADF Strategy has been identified as a potential core element of the Purdue Strategy implementation plan
As part of the initial assessment process, 8 products have been identified as potential components of the ADF Strategy

Purdue would like to assess the commercial potential of an ADF Strategy and evaluate the feasibility of capturing sustainable revenue and profit from the ADF Strategy
Concurrently, Purdue / Rhodes personnel are working on detailing the  development and clinical program costs for the potential ADF product opportunities

The costs will then be compared against the potential commercial opportunity to establish the overall viability of the ADF Strategy and develop next steps as needed
